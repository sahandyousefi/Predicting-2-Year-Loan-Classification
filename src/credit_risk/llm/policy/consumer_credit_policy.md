# Consumer Credit Policy (illustrative)

This policy was written for this project to test retrieval and grounded generation. It is not the policy of any real lender.

## POL-1 Scope and purpose
This policy covers unsecured personal loans and credit lines for individual borrowers. It sets eligibility rules, the use of the delinquency model, and the handling of declined applications.

## POL-2 Minimum eligibility
Applicants must be at least 18 years old, hold a valid identity document, and report a monthly gross income above zero that can be verified. Applications that fail these checks are declined before scoring.

## POL-3 Delinquency history
One payment 90 or more days past due in the last 24 months requires analyst review. Two or more such payments lead to a decline regardless of the model score. Payments 30 to 89 days past due are assessed through the model only.

## POL-4 Revolving utilization
Revolving utilization above 90 percent is referred to an analyst when the probability of default is within 5 percentage points of the cut-off. Utilization above 100 percent indicates balances over limit and must be noted in the case file.

## POL-5 Debt burden
The debt ratio, defined as monthly debt payments and living costs divided by monthly gross income, should not exceed 0.43 for a new unsecured loan. Exceptions require a documented compensating factor such as low utilization or significant verified savings.

## POL-6 Income verification
Income above 15,000 per month or self-employment income requires two documents, such as tax returns or bank statements. Unverified income is excluded from the debt ratio.

## POL-7 Model use and cut-offs
The probability of default from the approved model is compared with the cut-off set by the credit committee. Applications below the cut-off are approved, those above are declined, and those within 2 percentage points of the cut-off are referred to an analyst. The cut-off is reviewed each quarter against realized losses.

## POL-8 Adverse action notices
Every declined applicant receives a written notice within 30 days of the decision. The notice states up to four principal reasons taken from the model's reason codes, in order of importance, and explains how to request more detail.

## POL-9 Overrides
An analyst may override a model decision only with a documented reason. Override rates are reported monthly by analyst and direction, and an override rate above 5 percent of decisions triggers a review of the model and the cut-off.

## POL-10 Fair lending
Decisions must not be based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, receipt of public assistance, or the exercise of consumer rights. Age may be used only in an empirically derived scoring system and must not be assigned a negative value for applicants aged 62 or older. Approval rates by age band are monitored quarterly.

## POL-11 Model monitoring
Input stability is measured monthly with the Population Stability Index. A PSI above 0.25 on any key input or on the score triggers an investigation, and calibration is checked against observed default rates every quarter.
